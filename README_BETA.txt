Rail-Rider plinking rifle - 0.2.1 BETA (UMM)

A lever-action BB rifle for plinking, plus a cab rifle rack, six generic cans and a breakable green glass bottle.
Beta: expect rough edges. Hand-built props and no hand model. Includes the adult rifle model and 40 firing, cocking, shake and impact sound clips.

WHAT IS NEW IN 0.2.1
- Hold Shift while aiming for a subtle zoom (half of the normal right-click zoom). The camera near plane is pulled in while aiming so the rifle no longer clips.
- An empty rack can be taken off the wall again (it stays fixed while it holds a rifle).
- Rear sight is now level with the front blade and sits flush on the receiver.
- Wooden surfaces get their own impact sounds; the cocking stroke is back to the original ratchet.

WHAT WAS NEW IN 0.2.0
- New rack model: pine backing board with two steel hooks and leather linings. The rifle hangs muzzle-left with the carved stock side facing the cab.
- New rifle model and engraving (relief-lit wood, lever-action detail), new inventory art and icons for the rack, cans and bottle.
- Sampled sounds: cock, shot, shake rattles, and impact sounds that depend on what you hit (can, glass, locomotive, brick, ground, other).
- Cans and bottles are now litter near stations (not sold), can be binned in skips, and are never saved.
- Mod panel: Scatter toggle and "Clear all loose Rail-Rider items".

REQUIRES
- Unity Mod Manager
- Custom Item Mod 0.2.0 (id: custom_item_mod)

INSTALL
Drop the zip into Unity Mod Manager (or unzip the RailRider folder into <Derail Valley>\Mods). Start the game.
The rifle and the rifle rack are sold in the normal shops. The six cans and the green glass bottle are NOT sold: they turn up as litter on the ground and around bins near stations (and you can bin them in a dumpster like any other junk).

CONTROLS (rebindable in the mod's UMM panel; defaults are guesses that may clash with your bindings)
- Pick the rifle up as normal.
- Hold Shift while raised: subtle zoom
- Y  raise / lower the rifle (sights only, no hip fire)
- U  cock the lever (one stroke per shot; lowers the rifle first and returns to aim afterwards)
- Left click while raised and cocked: fire. Raised but uncocked gives a dry click.
- Shake / turn your view quickly and the rifle rattles. Drop or knock it and it rattles.
- Rack: place it on a cab surface with the normal gadget placement; with the rifle held and lowered, click the rack to hang it. Take it out by grabbing it.
- Debug spawn (can be switched off in the panel): F8 rifle at your feet plus a plinking line-up (6 random cans and 2 bottles) in front of you, Shift+F8 rack, F7 one of every can and a bottle in a row.

SAVES / CLEAN-UP
- Litter cans and bottles are never written to your save and are removed again when you walk away from them (unless you moved or shot them).
- Mod panel: "Scatter" toggle, and "Clear all loose Rail-Rider items" which deletes every loose rifle, rack, can and bottle. Racked rifles, attached racks, held items and inventory are not touched.

BEHAVIOUR
- 4.5 mm steel BB, 90 m/s, real air drag and gravity, zeroed for 15 m. It takes real time to arrive (about 0.18 s to 15 m).
- Hits push small props at the contact point (boosted well beyond real BB force for gameplay). Locos, heavy things and scenery only stop the shot.
- The glass bottle shatters when hit or knocked hard.

KNOWN ISSUES / PLEASE REPORT
- Rack: placement, rifle docking and save/load of a racked rifle are untested in the wild.
- Key defaults, aim pose and sight alignment may need tuning on other setups (FOV, resolution).
- Shop shelf and shop icons for the rifle are placeholders (inventory icon is final art).
- Please send Player.log (Derail Valley data folder) with any report. Turn on "Verbose log" in the mod panel first to get hit and shot details.
