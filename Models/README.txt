Rail-Rider starter assets

Files:
- rail_rider_rifle_placeholder.glb : complete placeholder rifle scene, meters, origin near receiver.
- rail_rider_body.obj : rifle body without moving lever.
- rail_rider_lever.obj : separate lever object for animation.
- steel_bb_4p5mm.glb : 4.5 mm diameter steel BB sphere.
- steel_bb_4p5mm.obj : same BB as OBJ.
- rail_rider_brand_plate.png : woodburn-style side graphic featuring a steam train and the Rail-Rider name.

Intended use:
- Keep rifle overall length around 0.90 m.
- Lever is separate so it can be rigged or rotated independently.
- BB radius is 0.00225 m (diameter 4.5 mm).

Suggested lever pivot:
- Approximately at receiver underside, around local position x=-0.028, y=0.0, z=-0.022 in the current placeholder layout.
- This placeholder is for blockout and code integration, not final art.
